Matlab code of the proposed free‑parameter Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)-based maximum power point tracking 
(MPPT) algorithm for photovoltaic (PV) systems operating under uniform irradiance conditions (UIC) and partial shading 
conditions (PSC). Unlike conventional and improved PSO variants that rely on velocity evolution and parameter tuning, the 
proposed method eliminates the velocity-update stage as well as the inertia and acceleration coefficients by introducing a 
dichotomy‑based midpoint contraction strategy. In the proposed PSO variant, each particle is iteratively moved toward the
swarm global-best solution through a direct position-update rule, resulting in a simplified optimization process with reduced 
computational complexity. To use this work or any part thereof for your research you are required to cite the paper: 
Arnaud NANFAK, Jean Blaise TEGUIA, René Constant FANJIP, Luc Vivien ASSIENE MOUODO, Samuel EKE, “A Free-parameter PSO-based MPPT algorithm using a dichotomy-based position 
update strategy for photovoltaic systems under partial shading conditions”, IET Renewable Power Generation, In review
